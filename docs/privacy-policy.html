Privacy Policy for FlyMate

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Last Updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FlyMate ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by CrazyNiro, collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: If you register an account, we may collect your email address, username, and authentication details.
  • Travel & Flight Information: Flight numbers, booking references, travel dates, departure/arrival airports, itinerary details, and custom notes you input to manage your trips.
  • Customer Support: Information you provide when contacting us for support or reporting issues.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, network state, and app crash logs (via diagnostic tools such as Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Usage Data: In-app interactions, feature usage, and session timestamps to help diagnose performance issues and improve user experience.

C. Information Not Collected

  • FlyMate does not track your real-time physical GPS location in the background.
  • FlyMate does not access your personal device contacts, microphone, camera, or biometric data unless explicitly requested for a specific feature with your runtime permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Core App Functionality: Storing and displaying your flight itineraries, calculating schedules, and sending relevant flight status updates.
  • Performance & Stability: Monitoring app health, investigating crashes, and pushing bug fixes.
  • Customer Communication: Responding to bug reports, feedback, and support inquiries.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with trusted third-party service providers strictly necessary to operate the App:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core platform dependencies and in-app updates.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for performance diagnostics, crash analytics (Crashlytics), and backend infrastructure.
  • Flight Data APIs: Used to retrieve airport and real-time flight telemetry (queries are limited to flight numbers/routes and do not contain personal identities).

All third parties process data under confidentiality obligations and comply with applicable data protection laws.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmitted between the App, third-party APIs, and back-end servers is encrypted in transit using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Locally cached application data is protected by Android’s application sandbox.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention: We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the services described in this policy, or until you request its deletion.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. To initiate a deletion request, email us at crazyniroapps@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Your request will be fulfilled within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

FlyMate is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our records.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, features, or relevant legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
